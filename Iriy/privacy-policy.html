IRIY

App: Iriy Last updated: November 16, 2026 Effective: November 16, 2026
This Privacy Policy describes how the Iriy application ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use it. Iriy is a client application: it connects to servers from configurations that are either delivered to the app through our backend or imported by you. We never have access to the contents of your traffic. This document explains what data the app collects, where it is stored, how long it is kept, and how to delete it.

01 Information We Collect

The app uses Google Firebase as its backend. To make it work, the following identifiers are created and stored on Firebase servers operated by Google:

  • Anonymous user ID — a random identifier generated by Firebase Authentication (anonymous sign-in) on first launch. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number, or Google account. We use it to associate your access to server configurations with your installation.
  • Installation ID and push token — generated by Firebase Installations / Firebase Cloud Messaging so the app can receive notifications about server availability.
  • App integrity tokens — short-lived tokens produced by Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity, used to verify that requests come from a genuine, unmodified copy of the app installed from Google Play. They are not stored by us.

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any credentials — there is no registration
  • Advertising ID, IMEI, or other hardware identifiers
  • Location data
  • Browsing history, DNS queries, visited domains, or traffic contents
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, or files beyond a configuration file you explicitly select
  • Usage analytics or crash reports

02 Your Traffic

When the tunnel is active, your network traffic is routed to the server from the configuration you selected — either one delivered through the app or one you imported. We do not log, store, inspect, or analyse your traffic in any way, and no information about your traffic is ever sent to Firebase or to us.

If you use a configuration you imported yourself, the operator of that server may be able to observe traffic passing through it. Please refer to the policy of whoever provides your configuration.

Server configurations delivered through the app are fetched from our backend (Firebase Cloud Firestore) and cached on your device. The backend records which anonymous user ID requested them, nothing more.

03 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The app stores the following data exclusively on your device, in its private application storage:

  • Configurations you import — server addresses, ports, protocol parameters and keys or passwords contained in your config file, link, or QR code. Stored in a local Room database.
  • Per-app routing rules — package names and labels of the apps for which you have chosen a routing mode (VPN / direct / auto). Only apps you explicitly change are saved.
  • Traffic counters — aggregate byte totals and speed samples shown in the app's statistics.
  • App settings — theme, language, and similar preferences, saved via Android DataStore.
  • Server country cache — the country code resolved for a server host, kept locally to avoid repeated lookups.

This data stays on your device and is excluded from Android cloud backup. It is removed when you delete the relevant profile, use Delete my data in the app settings, or uninstall the app.

You can also clear all local data at any time through your device's app settings:
Settings → Apps → Iriy → Clear Data

04 Network Requests & Third-Party Services

Apart from your own tunnel traffic, the app makes only the following requests:

  • Firebase (Google LLC) — our backend. Authentication issues the anonymous user ID; Cloud Firestore delivers server configurations, access settings, and in-app announcements; Cloud Messaging delivers notifications about server availability; App Check with Play Integrity verifies the app. Google processes this data on our behalf as a service provider. See the Firebase Privacy and Security page and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Subscription updates — if you add a subscription link, the app periodically fetches the configuration list from that URL, which you supplied. No other data is sent with the request.
  • Server country lookup (ipwho.is) — used only to display a country flag for a server. The request contains only the server's address, is made while the tunnel is active (so the service sees the server's IP, not yours), and the result is cached locally. No user traffic, app list, or routing rules are ever sent.
  • Connectivity check — after connecting, the app sends a small request through the tunnel to well-known connectivity-check endpoints to verify the connection actually works. It carries no personal data.

Your IP address may be visible to these services as part of standard internet communication; please refer to their own privacy policies for details.

05 Android Permissions

The app requests only the permissions it needs to function:

  • VPN service (BIND_VPN_SERVICE) — required to create the local tunnel interface. Granted by you through the system dialog and revocable at any time in Android settings.
  • Internet & network state — to establish the connection and detect network changes.
  • Foreground service — to keep the tunnel alive while the app is in the background, with a persistent status notification.
  • Notifications — connection status only. No promotional messages.
  • Camera — used solely to scan a configuration QR code, on the QR screen. No images are saved, stored, or transmitted.
  • Installed apps list — read locally to display the per-app routing screen. This list never leaves your device and is not transmitted anywhere.

06 No Advertising or Analytics

Iriy does not integrate any advertising networks, analytics SDKs (Google Analytics, Crashlytics, or similar), or social media trackers. Firebase is used only as the backend described in Section 04, not for analytics or advertising. No data is shared with any third party beyond the services listed there, and we never sell your data.

07 Data Retention & Deletion

The anonymous user ID and the data associated with it are kept on Firebase for as long as the app is installed and in use. Push tokens are refreshed automatically and old ones are discarded. App integrity tokens expire within hours and are not retained.

You can delete your data at any time:

  • In the app — Settings → Delete my data. This removes your record from our backend, deletes the anonymous account, invalidates the push token, and clears all local data. The action is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • By email — write to review4coolgames@gmail.com with the subject "Iriy data deletion". Because accounts are anonymous, please send the request from the device with the app installed, or include the user ID shown on the app's Settings screen. We process requests within 30 days.

Uninstalling the app removes all local data; the anonymous record on our backend is deleted on request as described above.

08 Open Source Components

Iriy uses the sing-box engine, distributed under the GNU General Public License v3. The corresponding source code and attribution are available in the app and in the project repository. Third-party components process data only on your device.

09 Children's Privacy

Iriy is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. The only identifiers the app creates are anonymous and are not linked to any person.
